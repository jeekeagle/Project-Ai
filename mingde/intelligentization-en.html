● Definition
What is Intelligentization?
AI and humans collaborate — AI replaces routine tasks, proactively provides analysis and advice, freeing human creativity. Its essence is collaboration — not replacement, but symbiosis that amplifies value.

Reduce burden = Improve efficiency: Replace routine + Free creativity
Do less, achieve more — free time for high-value constructive work

● Education Scenario Shifts
Intelligentization Shifts in Teaching Work
Create PPTs Manually → AI Drafts + Human Refines (AI creates draft, human reviews and personalizes)
Analyze Grades Manually → AI Alerts + Human Decides (AI scans data for anomalies, human judges intervention)
Write Lesson Plans Alone → AI-Assisted Lesson Planning (AI provides materials and frameworks, human focuses on design)
React to Problems → AI Proactive Alerts (AI monitors student status in real-time, pushes alerts proactively)
