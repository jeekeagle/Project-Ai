Where do you start today?

Intelligentization
智能化
1Use AI for lesson prep: feed your reflections as context

● Three Core Principles
Principles that apply across all three stages
📝
Accumulate First, Then Invoke
Without your own experience, AI output stays superficial. Build your knowledge base first, then let AI amplify it.
🤝
AI is an Amplifier
It doesn't replace your thinking — it amplifies your capability. Whatever you know, AI helps you scale.
🚀
Start Small, Start Today
No need to have everything in place. Start with one lesson reflection today and build from there.
