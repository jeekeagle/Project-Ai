Stage 01
● Definition
What is Informatization?
Moving information from the physical space into the digital space — from paper to the web, enabling fast information flow. Its essence is medium conversion.

Chalk → PPT    Paper lesson plans → Word docs    Physical files → Digital files

● Core Characteristics
Four Key Characteristics
📄
Medium ConversionPhysical world → digital space, storage medium changes
🔍
Information DisplacementInformation location changes, human actively retrieves
💬
Chat InterfaceHuman issues commands, system executes — human finds info
📄
No Essential ChangeInformation itself doesn't change, just relocated
● Education Scenario Shifts
Informatization Shifts in Teaching Work
Chalkboard → PPT (Teaching display migrates from blackboard to screen)
Paper Lesson Plans → Word Docs (Lesson planning shifts from handwritten to digital)
Physical Documents → Email / Digital Files (Document delivery shifts from courier to instant network)
Manual Research → Search Engines (Information retrieval shifts from books to keyword search)
● Representative Tools
Informatization Era Tools
This stage produced abundant foundational tools across teaching, management, and resource access.
📄 PowerPoint 📄 Word 📄 Excel 📥 Email 🔍 Search Engines 📚 Digital Libraries 💻 Computer Labs 🎥 Projectors 📷 Lecture Recording
● Limitations
Why Informatization Alone Falls Short
Informatization brought convenience, but has fundamental limits — which is why we must advance to digitalization and intelligentization.
✕ Information Silos: Systems not connected, data can't flow between them
✕ No Differentiation: Information itself doesn't change nature, just relocated
✕ Passive Retrieval: Info doesn't connect to user — human must search actively
✕ No Intelligence: Tools don't proactively think, judge, or advise
● Impact on Human Work
Human Work Status in the Informatization Era
● Still Dominant
Large amounts of routine execution still done manually — creating PPTs, organizing documents, entering data
● Limited Efficiency
Info access speed improved, but no proactive delivery or intelligent assistance — burden not truly reduced
● Medium Replacement
Teaching displays, lesson plans, and resource management go digital — but work method essence unchanged
● New Demands
Digital tool operation skills required — tool learning cost becomes a new burden
One-Line Summary
Informatization = Medium conversion, information displaced — humans still do most routine work
